Structured Recovery for At‑Risk Strategic Accounts

When a strategic account becomes unstable, the risk extends far beyond a single renewal — it threatens revenue, reputation, and internal alignment. Sentinel leads structured account recovery efforts that bring clarity, rebuild trust, and stabilize the relationship through renewal.

We align internal teams, diagnose root causes, and lead the recovery plan end‑to‑end so executives can protect the account and prevent future breakdowns.

Typical Engagements:
  • Strategic account stabilization and renewal protection
  • Root‑cause diagnostics across Product, Engineering, CS, and Sales
  • Executive‑level recovery leadership and communication
  • Post‑recovery operating improvements to prevent recurrence
