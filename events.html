Competition Divisions

MG Presentations 3:66 • We Set the Stage. You Set It Off!

Explore the official Dance Competition categories. Each division highlights creativity, precision, musicality, and unmatched energy.

  • Stands in Stands

    Coach‑led 2‑minute routine judged on creativity, synchronization, and crowd engagement.

  • Creative Solo

    A 2–3 minute solo judged on technique, musicality, stage presence, and creativity.

  • Quad Tag Team

    4‑minute routine with up to four dancers on the floor at once. High energy & rotation‑friendly.

  • Stand Battle

    90‑second rounds with DJ‑selected music. Judged on execution, confidence, and creativity.

  • Music Requirements

    Clean, radio‑edited music only. Teams provide music for all categories except Stand Battle.

  • General Rules

    Family‑friendly performances, sportsmanlike conduct, and prompt staging required. Judges’ decisions are final.
