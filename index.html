Booking Policies

Frequently Asked Questions

  • A non-refundable deposit is required to secure your appointment. Deposits are non-transferable and only refunded if the cancellation is due to The GLAM Lounge. Remaining balances are due at your appointment.

  • Please arrive 10–15 minutes early. A 10-minute grace period is allowed. After that, a late fee may apply. More than 15 minutes late may result in cancellation and a new deposit will be required.

  • You may reschedule once using your original deposit if proper notice is given. No-shows lose their deposit. Same-day cancellations may be treated as a no-show.

  • Please come alone unless approved. No extra guests. Children are not permitted unless they are receiving a service.

  • Arrive with a clean, makeup-free face. Removal of leftover makeup may result in a fee. Bring fresh lashes if preferred. Communicate allergies beforehand.

  • Arrive with hair prepared according to your service. Extra prep such as detangling, take-down, or washing may result in additional fees. Bring required wigs/hair unless discussed otherwise.

  • The appointment address will be provided according to booking instructions. Do not arrive early unless instructed.

  • Travel services start at $150 and vary by location. Travel fees must be paid according to booking requirements. Exact fees are confirmed before the appointment.

  • Photos or videos may be taken for portfolio and social media unless discussed otherwise. Notify the artist beforehand if you have concerns.

  • By booking, you confirm you have read and agreed to all policies. No exceptions will be made to clearly stated policies.
